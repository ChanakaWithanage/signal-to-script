Privacy notice

How optional signup and script downloads work here.

Optional email signup

When you join free, we store the email address you enter, a random internal member ID and the registration date in Cloudflare D1. Email ownership is not verified. We do not automatically subscribe you to marketing email.

Browser sessions and guest limits

An HTTP-only, secure member cookie enables access for up to 30 days. Only a hash of its random session token is stored in our database. Guests use a separate browser cookie to record one script download; it expires after 180 days. Clearing cookies or using another browser resets this limit.

We keep aggregate script download counts. The same Pine Scripts also remain publicly accessible in GitHub, so this is not a paid or private code access system.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts the site and database. Google AdSense on our pages and GitHub links may process information under their respective privacy notices.

Questions or removal

You can sign out on the free membership page, which ends your browser session but does not remove your email record. To request deletion, contact support and ask for a private channel. GitHub issues are public, so do not post your email address or other personal information there.
