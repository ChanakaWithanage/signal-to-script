Privacy Policy

How Signal to Script handles account access, site visits and service inquiries.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Information we collect

If you choose Google sign-in for unlimited downloads, Google Identity Services provides a verified Google account identifier and email address. We save these with account creation and last sign-in timestamps in Cloudflare D1. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Google Drive or trading accounts. Script downloads are counted in aggregate without linking individual downloads to member email addresses. You can also download one script as a guest without providing an email address. A random guest token is stored in a Secure, HttpOnly cookie and only its hash and the downloaded script identifier are stored in Cloudflare D1 to enforce the one-download limit.

Cloudflare may process IP addresses, browser data and request information to host and protect the website. When enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits. Google AdSense appears on the homepage and article pages, not sign-in or policy pages, and may use cookies or similar technology under Google's own policies.

Anonymous premium orders and interest counters

The premium checkout creates a random order secret stored in the purchasing browser. Cloudflare D1 stores only its hash, the purchase reference, script, selected currency, USD price, payment-provider order reference, order status and completed payment reference. A secret is required to download a purchased file, without registering a premium account. For checkout analytics, we also record the checkout attempt time in UTC and Sri Lanka time (UTC+05:30), an anonymous browser-tab session identifier, the selected script and payment method, and approximate country, region, city and time zone inferred by Cloudflare from the visitor's connection. The browser may provide its time zone. These location details are approximate and are not precise GPS location. We do not intentionally store the buyer's IP address in these D1 checkout analytics tables. NOWPayments processes crypto payments and PayPal processes PayPal checkout when enabled. Our server verifies completed payments with the respective payment provider before releasing private downloads. Blockchain transfers are public on the relevant network.

For the optional $4.99 manual lifetime membership request form, we collect the email address you enter, your selected Pine Script, the generated request text, the request timestamp, an anonymous checkout-session identifier, and approximate Cloudflare location information. We store these in Cloudflare D1 so we can reply with payment and delivery instructions. The form itself does not charge you or unlock a script. If email notifications are configured, we also send this request to our private support inbox through an email delivery provider, with your address as the reply-to contact. We do not use these inquiries to subscribe you to a marketing list. To request deletion, contact support@signaltoscript.com.

We also keep daily aggregate counts of premium article views, clicks on article links and clicks on Buy. These counters contain the date, product and action count, not email addresses or visitor IPs. They approximate interest and are not unique-human measurements.

How we use information

We use account details to authenticate you and enable free downloads. Technical information helps operate, protect and improve the website. If you contact us about custom software services, information you voluntarily provide is used to discuss and deliver that work. We do not subscribe you to marketing email simply because you sign in.

Paid services and payments

The free script library does not require payment. Premium Pine Script files may be purchased through NOWPayments cryptocurrency checkout or, when enabled, PayPal's hosted checkout. Payment-provider terms and privacy policies apply to those transactions. We do not store payment cards or PayPal passwords. We retain transaction references for download delivery, support, refunds, disputes and legal duties. If a separate custom development project is paid for with another provider such as PayHere, that provider processes the transaction under its own policies.

Cookies, security and retention

Google sign-in creates a Secure, HttpOnly browser session cookie, normally valid for up to 30 days. The guest download cookie is valid for up to 180 days, unless the browser clears it earlier. This is a browser-level limit and can be reset by removing cookies or using another browser. The server stores only a hash of its random session token in D1. Signing out ends your active session, but does not automatically delete your account record. We retain account and order details while needed for access, payment support and applicable recordkeeping duties, or until an appropriate deletion request is processed, subject to legal requirements. Checkout-attempt analytics containing approximate location and an anonymous session ID are retained for operational analysis and can be deleted on request when their records can be located. We use reasonable security measures, though no online service can promise absolute security.

Gold Breakout strategy access

When you activate free Gold Breakout alerts, we store the email you enter, your first and most recent signup dates, the number of signup requests associated with that address, the seven-day access window and your notification preferences in Cloudflare D1. Signup starts immediately without requiring email verification. We also retain the page or campaign that brought you to the workspace, your browser time zone, and approximate country, region and city when Cloudflare makes them available. We do not intentionally store your IP address in this subscriber table. A secure, HttpOnly browser cookie keeps the session active. Entering an email alone does not prove ownership, so lifetime membership access requires an existing verified sign-in or a completed private payment receipt.

If you choose email alerts, Resend is used to deliver strategy notifications. If you connect Discord, the webhook address is encrypted before storage; Telegram connections store the chat ID needed to deliver alerts. You can change notification settings from the workspace. After five days we may show you an optional membership offer. You can ask us to correct or delete subscriber records by emailing support@signaltoscript.com.

Service providers

Cloudflare provides website hosting, D1 database services and, when configured, private file storage and analytics. NOWPayments processes cryptocurrency checkout and payment notifications. PayPal processes PayPal orders when that option is enabled. Google provides authentication and advertising on content pages. GitHub hosts linked code and public issue discussions. When configured, Resend transmits a copy of manual script purchase requests to our support inbox. Payment providers may be used for separately agreed paid services. We do not sell registered email addresses. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Your choices and contact

You can sign out on the free sign-in page. To ask questions, correct your account details or request deletion, email support@signaltoscript.com. Please do not email passwords or full payment card details.

This policy may be updated as our services change. Check the date above for the latest revision. See our Terms & Conditions and Refund & Cancellation Policy.
