Privacy notice

What happens when you sign in to download free scripts.

Google sign-in and account permissions

When you choose Google sign-in, Google shares a verified account identifier and email address with us. Our server verifies the signed Google ID token, then stores your Google account identifier, email and login timestamps in Cloudflare D1. We never receive your Google password and do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or your trading accounts. There is no forced marketing newsletter.

Sign-in sessions and downloads

An HTTP-only, secure member cookie enables access for up to 30 days. Only a hash of its random session token is stored in our database. Returning users can sign in with Google again on another device.

We keep aggregate script download counts without attaching member emails to download counts.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts the site and database. Google Identity Services processes sign-in. Google AdSense runs on the homepage and content articles, but not on the join or privacy pages. Google services and linked GitHub pages may process information under their own privacy notices.

Questions or removal

You can sign out on the free membership page, which ends your browser session but does not remove your email record. To request deletion, contact support and ask for a private channel. GitHub issues are public, so do not post your email address or other personal information there.
