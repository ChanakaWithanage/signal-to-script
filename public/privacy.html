Privacy Policy

How Signal to Script handles account access, site visits and service inquiries.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Information we collect

For free downloads, Google Identity Services provides a verified Google account identifier and email address. We save these with account creation and last sign-in timestamps in Cloudflare D1. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Google Drive or trading accounts. Script downloads are counted in aggregate without linking individual downloads to member email addresses.

Cloudflare may process IP addresses, browser data and request information to host and protect the website. When enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits. Google AdSense appears on the homepage and article pages, not sign-in or policy pages, and may use cookies or similar technology under Google's own policies.

How we use information

We use account details to authenticate you and enable free downloads. Technical information helps operate, protect and improve the website. If you contact us about custom software services, information you voluntarily provide is used to discuss and deliver that work. We do not subscribe you to marketing email simply because you sign in.

Paid services and payments

The published free Pine Script downloads do not require a payment. If a separately agreed development project is paid for using a provider such as PayHere, that provider processes payment information under its own policies. We do not store full card details on this website. A transaction reference and related correspondence may be retained when needed to handle delivery, support, refunds, disputes or legal duties. PayHere checkout is not currently part of the free download flow.

Cookies, security and retention

Google sign-in creates a Secure, HttpOnly browser session cookie, normally valid for up to 30 days. The server stores only a hash of its random session token in D1. Signing out ends your active session, but does not automatically delete your account record. We retain account details while needed for access and operation, or until an appropriate deletion request is processed, subject to legal requirements. We use reasonable security measures, though no online service can promise absolute security.

Service providers

Cloudflare provides website hosting, D1 database services and, when configured, private file storage and analytics. Google provides authentication and advertising on content pages. GitHub hosts linked code and public issue discussions. Payment providers may be used for separately agreed paid services. We do not sell registered email addresses. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Your choices and contact

You can sign out on the free sign-in page. For corrections, account deletion or questions, contact Signal to Script support and request a private channel. GitHub Issues are public: do not post your email, payment references or other personal details in a public issue.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as our services change. Check the date above for the latest revision. See our Terms & Conditions and Refund & Cancellation Policy.
