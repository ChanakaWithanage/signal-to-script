My Gold Breakout Lost $198. Then I Moved the Clock.

When I laid out four years of Gold Breakout tests, the 2019 column was the one I kept coming back to. The other three years were positive. This one had lost $197.94. Before touching the stop or adding another filter, I wanted to see what happened if I changed when the strategy started watching gold.

By CW · November 23, 2026 · Strategy research

At 07:00, the two versions start doing different things

On the 15-minute chart, the original version has just finished marking the overnight high and low. Its breakout orders can go live. The morning version is only now starting to build its range. If gold runs through the overnight high at 08:00, that move can trigger the original. For the morning version, that same move helps set the high it will watch after 11:00.

That changes both the price that can trigger an entry and when the first trade can happen. The morning test also moves the scheduled exit from 20:00 to 21:00. The one-entry-per-day rule and the 1% stop stay the same.

Read the full entry and exit rules in the Gold Breakout 15M strategy guide.

The 2019 loss flipped. Would the settings also hold up in 2023?

The morning version turned the 2019 result from a $197.94 loss into a $47.76 gain. The same configuration made $100.67 in 2018 and $359.31 in 2022. I used those three years to select it, then checked the same settings in 2023. It made $52.12 there, compared with $31.51 for the original.

The drawdown changed what I’d compare next

The morning version had the higher four-year net of these three settings. It also had the deepest drawdown in any single test year, at $340.82 with one-ounce sizing. The 08:00–18:00 window had a smaller net sum, at $284.65, and its largest annual drawdown was $65.83. I’d put both in the next test. One gives me a higher-net benchmark. The other gives me a much lower drawdown to compare it with.

How the comparison was run

I screened 1,620 schedules and validated 28 configurations against the Pine trade logic in PineTS. The runs used Dukascopy XAUUSD BID 15-minute candles for 2018, 2019, 2022 and 2023. Each year started independently at $300 with fixed one-ounce sizing and a modeled $0.30 round-trip spread per trade, with no additional commission. Four-year net sums add the individual year results.
