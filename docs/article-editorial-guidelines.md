# Signal to Script article guidelines

- Start with a specific moment and a decision the reader can follow. Show what the script surfaced, why it happened, what the reader could do with it, and what happened next.
- Use recent, verified market examples where the topic allows. Give charts a job in the story. Label historical reconstructions and backtest assumptions in the caption or a short methodology section.
- Use plain, natural copy, short paragraphs, and a clear reason to keep reading. Keep purchase calls to action focused on the script and what access includes. Skip generic warnings and filler.
- Match the existing site’s typography, spacing, and navigation. Check chart readability, table fit, and call-to-action placement on desktop and mobile before publishing.
